## Notes and skipped words

- Skipped as doubtful or without an unambiguous picture/meaning: `bat` (animal vs baseball bat), `ball`/`football` picture (soccer vs American football), `orange` picture is the colour circle (not the fruit), `bag`, `pants`, `doctor`/`farmer`/`nurse` pictures (no single-codepoint emoji), `jacket`, `letter`, `chicken` (animal vs food), `cup`, `bowl`, `ghost`-type scary topics, and abstract words with doubtful dictionary hints (a, above, action, activity, ...).
- `old` is taught only in "How old are you?" (age), `nose` gloss はな refers to the face part (hana, 鼻), `flower` はな (花).
- `brother` is glossed as the child's male sibling; the child-facing question only uses it in "He is my brother."
- The phrase "You're welcome." and the name "Ken" are used as everyday dialogue phrases although `welcome`/`Ken` are not in words-5.json.

## Emoji caveats

- Colour words (black, blue, brown, green, orange) use coloured-circle emoji; `orange` is the colour circle, NOT the fruit.
- `hat` uses the woman sun-hat emoji, `cap` the billed cap; no unambiguous plain-hat emoji exists.
- `boat` uses the speedboat emoji (gloss ボート／ふね).
- Dialogue items with a picture (cat, clock, blue circle) let the reply be derived.
