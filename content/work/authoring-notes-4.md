## Skipped as doubtful, abstract or unpicturable

- Words with a doubtful dictionary hint (`jaLookup: doubtful`, see review-ja-4.json) were taught only when the item itself makes the meaning unambiguous (`fall` = autumn, `bill` = restaurant bill, `cost` = price); the rest were not used (for example `point`, `match`, `case`, `set`, `type`, `pool`, `host`, `issue`).
- Skipped for child-safety: `beer`, `champagne`, `cigarette`, `smoke`, `bra`, `gun`, `killer`, `kill`, `death`, `dead`, `die`, `war`, `blood`, `crime`, `drug`, `curse`, `invade`, `horror`, `attack`, `bride` (wedding/romance), `kiss`.
- Pictures not used (ambiguous or no emoji): `basket`, `fork`, `golf`, `cricket` (sport or insect), `globe`, `bug`/`insect`, `lamp`, `chef`/`astronaut`/`dentist` (person emoji need joined sequences), `ham`, `jam`, `jelly`, `comb`, `bench`.

## Emoji caveats

- 27 new Twemoji (v17.0, CC-BY 4.0): balloon, battery, calendar, carrot, castle, chess, clown, coin, crown, diamond, dinosaur, duck, earth, elephant, envelope, garlic, glove, honey, hockey, badminton, feather, camping, desert, angel, curry, headphone, fog.
- `chess` is the black pawn; `coin` a gold coin; `earth` the globe showing Europe and Africa; `envelope` a plain closed envelope; `dinosaur` is a T-rex (the long-necked one is a different emoji); `headphone` is over-ear headphones; `angel` is a baby angel with wings; `desert` is a desert landscape (sand, cactus, sun); `curry` is curry and rice; `hockey` is an ice-hockey stick and puck; `camping` is a tent; `diamond` is a blue gem; `badminton` is a racket with a shuttlecock; `fog` is a layered fog cloud; `feather` a single feather; `honey` a honey pot; `duck` a white-and-yellow duck (never paired with the `bird` picture).
- A `bride` emoji was downloaded and then removed (wedding/romance topic).
- In dialogue-choice items a picture is only used where the answer needs it; yes/no items with a picture ("Is it a dog?") rely on the picture being clearly an elephant.

Fix round 1: `avoid`, `hate` dropped (replaced by `gentle`; the hate dialogue now teaches `carrot`); `crazy` is only used for ideas ("a crazy idea"), never for a person; `fall` is taught as autumn only ("leaves turn red in ___").
